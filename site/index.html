      What the labels mean

      New
      Arrived since your last visit. On your first visit: the 24 hours before the latest update. The New since last visit view lists exactly these.
      Unread
      Not opened or marked read yet, however old. A blue dot sits beside the headline.
      Read
      The dot goes, the headline fades and the ring fills with a check. Opening an item marks it read.
      Saved
      Starred items stay in Saved, even after they leave the 7-day feed.
      term
      An amber row with this badge mentions one of your watchlist terms.
      Just arrived
      Landed while this page was open. The blue pill above the timeline shows them (or press n).

      Searching

      Every word must appear, as a whole word, in the headline, summary or source name: APT2 won’t find APT28. End a word with * to catch longer ones (ransom* finds ransomware), and put a phrase in "quotes" to keep its words together. Watchlist terms follow the same rule. Accents and capitals don’t matter.
