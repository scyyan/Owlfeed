      What the labels mean

      New
      Arrived since your last visit. On your first visit: the last 24 hours. The New since last visit view lists exactly these.
      Unread
      Not opened or marked read yet, however old.
      Just arrived
      Landed while this page was open. The blue pill above the timeline shows them (or press n).

      Searching

      Every word must appear, as a whole word, in the headline, summary or source name: APT2 won’t find APT28. End a word with * to catch longer ones (ransom* finds ransomware), and put a phrase in "quotes" to keep its words together. Watchlist terms follow the same rule. Accents and capitals don’t matter.
